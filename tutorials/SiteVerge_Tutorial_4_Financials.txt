Episode four. Financial analysis.
Financial Analysis starts with three numbers: land price, construction cost, and rent. SiteVerge estimates all three from public data.
Got a real asking price? Type it in. Here, $650,000.
Hit Use, and the whole model reruns. The net present value drops from $746K to $596K.
The summary shows the big ones: total cost, income, value, yield on cost, and returns.
Scenarios stress-test it: conservative, base, and optimistic. In the conservative case, this one doesn't pencil, and that's worth knowing.
Returns show the investor's cash flow, year by year. Money goes in early, and the big payoff comes at the sale.
In Advanced, drag the sliders for exit cap, interest rates, and vacancy, and see how the returns respond.
Then Monte Carlo runs the model 1,000 times. Here, about 61% of runs come out positive. That's a range, not a promise.
