Episode one. Searching a city.
This is SiteVerge. It's free, there's no account, and it works for any city in the United States.
Choose Find sites in a city, type a city and state. We'll try Charlotte, North Carolina. Then hit Find lots.
Now SiteVerge goes to work. It ranks every neighborhood, pulls the county's parcel records, and throws out anything with a building, no street access, or a pond on it.
Seconds later, here are the lots that passed the screen. Ranked on the left, pinned on the map.
Up top, building type and max floors control what gets tested. Change them, press Run, and it searches again.
Want more control? Open Assumptions. Filters set your limits: land cost, flood risk, slope, and the smallest lot you'd consider.
Massing sets the building rules: floor area ratio, setbacks, and how much is residential. Where SiteVerge knows the zoning, the real limits take over.
And Weights decide what matters most to you. Slide them around, and the ranking reshuffles instantly.
